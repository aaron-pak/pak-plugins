Shakshuka in one pan, in thirty minutes

Eggs set gently in a spiced tomato sauce. Most of the time is the sauce simmering, so it's a good one to cook while you talk.

Method

  1. Soften the onion and pepper

    Oil in the pan over a medium heat. Cook them with a pinch of salt until soft and sweet, about 8 minutes. Don't rush this; it's where the flavour comes from.

  2. Add the garlic and spices

    One minute, stirring, until it smells toasty.

  3. Simmer the tomatoes

    Tip them in, rinse the can with a splash of water, and simmer for 10 minutes until thick enough that a spoon leaves a trail. Taste for salt.

  4. Set the eggs

    Make a well for each egg and crack it in. Lid on, heat low, 6 to 8 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks still move.

  5. Finish

    Feta and parsley over the top. Bring the pan to the table with the bread.

If the whites won't set

Spoon some of the bubbling tomato over the whites, lid back on, one more minute. Turning the heat up only hardens the yolks.
