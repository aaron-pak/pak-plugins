# Callout

A note set apart on a soft ground: a tip, a warning, a blocker, a right or wrong answer.

- `<aside class="sk-callout" data-tone="clay|warn|good|bad">` with an optional spot, an optional `.sk-label` (coloured by the tone), an `h4` that says the point, and a short paragraph.
- A warning, and every toned callout in dark mode, has a coloured edge: a straight bar down the left side, and the two corners it touches are square. An edge never bends round a rounded corner.
- No tone is the neutral panel. Tones mean what they say: warn for a risk, bad for a failure, good for right or done, clay for the one takeaway.
- One or two per page; a page of callouts has none.
