Wharf Lane costs a quarter less for its size than the middle flat we saw, and it is the only one with a second bedroom big enough for both desks. The catch is the 25-minute walk to the station.2 If that walk is fine on a wet Tuesday, it is the one to take.

What the numbers miss

The walk, the noise, and a landlord who wants to stay

We walked to the station from each flat at 8 am on a weekday rather than trusting the map. Wharf Lane's 25 minutes is mostly along the canal, which is pleasant in October and less so in February. The 12 bus cuts it to 15 minutes, every 10 minutes at rush hour.

Hill Court is closest to everything and loudest: the bedroom faces the bus stop. Albion St is lovely and too small once both of us work from home.

Walk the route at 8 am in the rain before we sign. Twenty-five minutes on a wet morning is a different flat.

What we'd do next

Three things this week, then sign

  1. Walk it in bad weather

    Wednesday's forecast is rain. Leave at 7:55 and time the walk and the 12 bus.

  2. Ask for the two-year lease in writing

    At £1,520 a month for both years, with the shed included.

  3. Measure for the sofa

    The stairs turn at the first landing. If it won't go up, we sell it rather than lose the flat.
