# Title lockup

The top of every page: a mono `.sk-label`, a Bricolage `.sk-title`, a Literata `.sk-lede`.

- The title states the point or asks the question, in sentence case: "Sales are up 12%, and mornings did most of it", never "Sales overview".
- The label says where you are (a series, a date, a status). The lede is one or two sentences, under 50ch.
- `data-size="xl"` on the title for a launch or tour page with a centred hero. No italic word and no squiggle. One phrase may take a hand underline (see Hand marks) when it is the point of the page.
