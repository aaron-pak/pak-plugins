How to find one name in a million with twenty looks

Open a sorted list in the middle and throw away the half the name can't be in. Do it again with what's left. Step through it below, on sixteen names.

Why it's fast

Everyone on Earth takes 33 looks

Doubling the list adds one look. A thousand names take at most ten looks, a million at most twenty, and every person alive fits in thirty-three.

It only works on a list that's already in order

Sorting takes far longer than one read through. For a single search of an unsorted list, just read it from the top. Sort once when you'll search it many times.
