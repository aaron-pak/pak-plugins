People who have heard it
  1. Day 1: one person hears it. Tomorrow they tell two friends, then stop.
  2. Day 2: three people know. The two who just heard each tell two more tomorrow.
  3. Day 3: seven. Four new people today, and each of them will tell two.
  4. Day 4: fifteen. Eight new today. Each day the number of new people doubles.
  5. Day 5: thirty-one. Sixteen new. Most people who know heard it today or yesterday.
  6. Day 6: sixty-three. Thirty-two heard it today, more than in all five days before put together.
