# Two by two

Sort things on two axes: effort against payoff, likelihood against impact, what you decided against what was true.

- `<div class="sk-quad">`, then `<p class="sk-quad-y">` with the low and high ends of the vertical axis, four `div.sk-quad-cell` (top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right), then `<p class="sk-quad-x">` with the left and right ends.
- The top right cell is the one that matters and sits on clay-soft; the others stay open on the paper, divided by two ink rules that are the axes. Name each cell for what to do with what's in it ("Do these", "Skip"), not for its position.
- The vertical axis reads bottom to top, like a chart. Give the box a height when its cells are short.
