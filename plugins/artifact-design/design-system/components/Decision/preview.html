A recommendation · November 2026

Keep the family photos in iCloud, and one copy at a friend's house

We have 1.6 TB of photos and they're only on one Mac. Three ways to fix that, what each costs, and what each survives.

The options

Three ways to keep two copies

iCloud+ and a drive

$9.99a month, plus a $90 drive once

Photos sync from every phone on their own. Once a year, copy everything to a drive that lives at Sam's place.

  • Nothing to set up on the phones
  • Survives a fire or a theft
  • One yearly chore you have to remember

A home server and Backblaze

$9.60a month for 1.6 TB, plus $480 once

A small server at home holds everything and backs itself up to Backblaze every night.

  • Fully ours, no account to lose
  • Pay only for what you store, $6 a TB
  • A weekend to set up
  • Someone has to keep it running

Google One

$9.99a month

Google Photos backs up every phone. Same price as iCloud for 2 TB.

  • Best search of the three
  • The Macs need a separate app
  • No copy outside Google

Why this one

The account is the risk, so keep a copy outside it

Every option survives a dead Mac or a fire. What only the first two survive is losing the account itself, which happens more often than people think: a forgotten password, a payment that fails, a flag on the account. A drive in a drawer at a friend's house fixes that for $90.

Set a reminder

First Sunday of January: plug in the drive, let it copy overnight, take it back to Sam's.

Before we pay

What would change my mind

If we want nothing in the cloud

Then the home server is the only option that fits, and the setup weekend is worth it.

If the family moves to Android

Google One becomes the easy choice. The drive at Sam's still makes sense either way.
