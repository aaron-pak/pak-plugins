# Hand marks

One clay brush line round a number or under a phrase, the way you would mark up a printout. It draws itself a beat after its text arrives.

- `<span data-sk-under>the phrase</span>`: a brush line under the words. It follows them when they wrap.
- `<span data-sk-ring>20–50</span>`: a loop round a number or a short word. Give it room: nothing within half a line above or below.
- Mark what the reader should leave with: the claim in a title, the number that matters, the key phrase in a paragraph. One mark in a view, and two or three on a page.
- Never as decoration, never on a link or a button, and never two marks on one phrase.
