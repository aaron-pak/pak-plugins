How this is worked out

The loan payment uses the standard formula for a fixed-rate loan: the rate per month, the number of months, and the amount borrowed. Tax and insurance are your yearly numbers divided by twelve.

Mortgage insurance is added while the loan is above 80% of the price, which is when most lenders drop it.
